  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lucide-icons/lucide/1d7da164bbaf684159accdfd3d568d82b87d54bd/icons/<source>.svg`

change `stroke-width="2"` to `stroke-width="2.75"`; `key-round.svg` also drops
one child `fill="currentColor"` to enforce the no-fill contract.
Renamed upstream sources: `triangle-alert.svg` is delivered as
`alert-triangle.svg`, `ellipsis-vertical.svg` as `more-vertical.svg`, and
`fingerprint-pattern.svg` as `fingerprint.svg`.
